Filled with about 60 ton of molten iron.

A blast of oxygen is blown through the converter as oxygen reacts with the impurities and raises the temperature in the furnace to about 1900°C, Temperature control CO2 and cold scrap metal C is oxidized to CO, which burns to CO2 at the top of the converter. 

After several minutes, the flame at the top of the converter sinks, indicating that the conversion occurred. Si is oxidized to SiO2, which then reacts with the oxides of other
elements to form a slag. The furnace also is lined with limestone (CaCO3), which reacts
with acidic phosphorus-containing impurities. At the end, the slag is poured off, and any required trace elements are added to the molten iron. For normal steel, between 0.1 and 1.5 percent carbon is required.
