Zinc sulfide is an inorganic compound with the chemical formula of ZnS. It can exist in two different structures: sphalerite and wurtzite. In sphalerite the Zn2+ ions occupy tetrahedral holes in a ccp lattice and in wurtzite the Zn2+ ions occupy a tetrahedral holes hcp lattice. Use the Born-Landé equation to calculate the lattice energies (kJ/mol) of sphalerite, Born exponent _n_ = _9_.

For sphalerite, A = 1.638 so 
U = –\[(1.389×10–4 J m/mole)(+2)(–2)(1.638)/(258×10–12 m)(1 – 1/9)] = -3137 kJ/mol.

Use the Born-Landé equation to calculate the lattice energies (kJ/mol) of wurtzite. Born exponent _n_ = _9_.

For wurtzite, A = 1.641 so 
U = \[(1.389×10–4 J m/mole)(+2)(–2)(1.641)/(258×10–12 m)(1 – 1/9)] = -3143 kJ/mol.
