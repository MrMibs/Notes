$B_0$ is the external static magnetic field applied in NMR experiments.

It determines:
- Larmor frequency: $\omega_0 = \gamma B_0$
- Energy level splitting (Zeeman effect) (Det med I og $\Delta E$)
- Overall sensitivity and resolution of the spectrum

Higher $B_0$ → larger frequency separation and better spectral resolution.
