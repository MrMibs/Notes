Chemicals Required: 
1) Iron Sulfate Heptahydrate 
2) Ferric Chloride Hexahydrate 
3) Hydrochloric Acid 
4) Ammonium Hydroxide 
5) Tetramethylammonium Hydroxide (TMAH) 6) Deionized Water (DI Water)

Instruments Utilized: 
1) Corning PC-620D 
2) ZD-9556 Orbital Shaker 
3) Scilogex OS20-Pro

### Step 1: Prepairing precursors
**Blue sulfate solution**: 
2.78g of iron sulfate heptahydrate
5ml DI water
2M solution

**Brown chloride solution**:
5.40g ferric chloride heptahydrite
20 ml DI water
1M solution

Orbital shaker at 120rpm for 20 min to form homogenous solutions. Brown chloride solution stirred at 200RPM. Add blue sulfate solution and stir for another 5.

### Step 2: Introducing the acidic solution
Always add acid to the water, not the other way around

Add the metallic stirring rod and 16ml of DI-water. Stir at 300RPM. Slowly add 8.33mL of HCl to attain 25mL of 4M solution. 

Add to the solution above with pipette and stir for 5 minutes. Color turns yellow.

## Step 3: Introducing the ammonium hydroxide
225.67 mL of DI water for the solution base and add stirring rod. Stir at 300 RPM. Add 24.33 mL of ammonium hydroxide and allow to stir thoroughly achieving a 25 mL of a 0.7M solution.

Gently add this to the solution so far and it turns black. Set to 600 RPM and stir for 30 min.

# Step 4: Cleaning
Place magnet beneath beaker to let particles form at the bottom. This also checks that it works and you have made them. Carefully dispose of excess liquid using pipette:
![[Pasted image 20260612120809.png]]
Replace stirring rod and set speed to 300 RPM. Add 50 to 100 ml of DI water to rinse, don't let it splash. Stir for 5 minutes. Remove excess liquid. Do this 3 more times.
