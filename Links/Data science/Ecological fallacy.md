## Definition
Assuming that relationships observed for groups also apply to individuals within those groups.

## Why it is a problem
Group-level trends can hide individual-level variation.

## Example
Countries with higher average income have longer life expectancy.
→ It does **not** follow that every wealthy person lives longer than every poorer person.

## Warning signs
- Conclusions about individuals are drawn from aggregated data.
- Data are summarized by regions, schools, countries, etc.

## How to avoid it
- Use individual-level data when making claims about individuals.
- Distinguish group-level and individual-level conclusions.

## Key idea
Group patterns are not necessarily individual patterns.
