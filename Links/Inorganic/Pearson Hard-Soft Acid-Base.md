#inorganic 
Hard: metal cations (Lewis acids) small, compact and not very polarizable, prefer bases that are also small and less polarizable anions (Lewis bases).

Soft: metal ions (Lewis acids) with low charge density and no so low electronegativity. These acids tend to form complexes with larger and more polarizable Lewis bases.

The empirical relationship: hard acids prefer hard bases and soft acids prefer soft bases.
