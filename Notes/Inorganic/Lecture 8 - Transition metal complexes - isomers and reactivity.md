#inorganic
Continuing from [[Crystal field theory]] we have Ligand field theory.
Metal orbitals are divided according to the symmetry;
- The orbitals of the ligands are combined to give linear combinations,
which are of the correct symmetry to interact with the s, p and some of
the d orbitals of the central metal;
- This is a much more correct approach than crystal field theory, but is
not as easy to use.
