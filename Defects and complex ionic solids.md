Defects exist as either Schottky defects with missing ions, Frenkel Defects with ions in interstitial sites. 

Further, extrinsic defects i.e. defects in a crystal lattice caused by the **presence of impurity atoms (foreign species)** rather than missing or misplaced atoms of the host material exist such as non stoichiometric compounds. These are solids where the **ideal integer ratio of elements is not strictly obeyed**, often because of defects and here the crystal stays neutral by adjusting oxidation states or defect concentrations. 

Or doping, which is the deliberate introduction of **foreign atoms into a crystal** to change its properties. This creates extrinsic charge carriers & strongly changes conductivity (especially in semiconductors)
