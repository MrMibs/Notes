## Definition
Non-stationarity means the statistical properties of data change over time.

## Core idea
The relationship between variables is not stable across time or environments.

## Example
- Consumer behavior changes after a pandemic
- Fraud patterns evolve over time
- Climate data shifts across decades

## Why it is a problem
- Models trained on past data become outdated
- Predictions degrade over time

## Key intuition
The future is not generated by the same distribution as the past.

## When it matters
- Time series forecasting
- Finance
- Recommendation systems
