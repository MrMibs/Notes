## Granger Causality
## Meaning
Tests whether past values of X help predict Y.

## Key idea
If X improves prediction of future Y → X “Granger-causes” Y.

---

## Instrumental Variables (IV)
## Meaning
Method to estimate causal effects when confounding exists.

## Requirements
- affects X
- does not directly affect Y
- only influences Y through X

## Key idea
Used to isolate causal variation in observational data.
