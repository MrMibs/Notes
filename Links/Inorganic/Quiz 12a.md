#inorganic 

In the synthesis reported in the article, \[water] micro-droplets are dispersed in \[cyclohexane]. This water in \[oil] (w/o) emulsion is stabilized by a surfactant. Indeed, \[Brij‑97] is a commercial non-ionic surfactant, whose molecular structure consists of a hydrophobic \[C18H35] chain connected to a hydrophilic \[(OCH2CH2)nOH] group.  The surfactant will lay at the water/oil interphase with the \[hydrophilic] part interacting with water and the \[hydrophobic] part interacting with cyclohexane. \[Water] droplets behave as micro-reactors in which the the magnetite precursors react to form magnetite \[nanoparticles]. The size of the nanoparticles will depend on the size of the \[water droplets] and on the \[concentration] of the precursors.
