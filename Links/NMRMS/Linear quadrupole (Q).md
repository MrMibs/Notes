We keep the AC to DC ratio consistant, (following the line marked with const. at the bottom of the red part). The stable area is then what is drawn within the red area. If we want to increase which ions we accept we can lower our resolution by decreasing field strength and if we want to increase our resolution we can increase it but then we lose more ions.

This is perfect! We vary this during a scan. As we know the low m/z will be the least affected and fly off the slowest, we can get them through first when we filter away most things, then we can slowly increase the frequency of the AC to allow more things through, thereby being able to filter for m/z ratio.
