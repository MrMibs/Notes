#datascience
## 1) Exploratory plots

Used to **figure out what is in the data**

- messy is fine
- many variables at once
- lots of layers, colors, facets
- no need for polished presentation

Purpose: _discover patterns_

---

## 2) Explanatory plots

Used to **communicate a conclusion**

- simplified and clean
- only relevant variables shown
- annotations, labels, focus
- designed for others to understand

Purpose: _tell a story_
