#P5 
This article 3D printed spacers for membrane distillation (MD) applications. The best performing TPMS spacer design was identified to have the highest surface area to volume ratio along with a design structure that caused relatively higher turbulence by disrupting the feed flow.

Particle deposition tests were done using microspheres to visualize the impact of TPMS spacer design on dead zone formation. Pearson correlation coefficient showed that [[Particle deposition]] (surface attachment) is strongly correlated to the spacer voidage and its membrane contact area.
