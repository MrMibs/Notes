#nmrms
Accuracy measures accuracy of the mass measurement, measure of [[Instrument performance]], illustrated below: 
![[Pasted image 20260528121403.png]]
Decimal count will be specified. In an experiment, differences between exact and experimental masses can occur for a multitude of reasons including but not limited to poor calibration, low resolution and so on.
![[Pasted image 20260528121557.png]]

Absolute accuracy is found as
$$Absolute \, accuracy = m/z_{experimental}-m/z_{calculated}$$
However relative accuracy is typically used as MS is so accurate
$$Relative \, accuracy = \frac{m/z_{experimental}-m/z_{calculated}}{m/z_{calculated}}\cdot 10^6ppm$$
Precision is also used as a measure of [[Instrument performance]] and describes the deviation of the experimental value from the true value. It is an expression of random error such as calibration, drift and so on. Precision vs accuracy:
![[Pasted image 20260528122303.png]]
