To predict bond geometry we need hybridization. We know:
- A covalent bond is formed by pairing the unpaired electrons in neighboring atoms
- The spins of the unpaired electrons must be antiparallel
![[Screenshot 2026-06-09 121018(1).png]]

To provide enough unpaired electrons in each atom for the maximum bond formation, electrons can be excited to fill empty orbitals during bond formation.
