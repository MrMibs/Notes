#inorganic 
We combine [[Crystal field theory]] with [[Molecular Orbitals Theory]]:

Metal orbitals are divided according to the symmetry;
- The orbitals of the ligands are combined to give linear combinations, which are of the correct symmetry to interact with the s, p and some of the d orbitals of the central metal;
- This is a much more correct approach than crystal field theory, but is not as easy to use.

![[Pasted image 20260611131944.png]]

In reality we have different energy levels:
![[Pasted image 20260611132034.png]]

For these energies we have some rules:
Spin Rule: transitions must involve the promotion of electrons without a change
in their spin.
Laporte rule: transitions between states of the same symmetry with respect to the
inversion center are forbidden.
