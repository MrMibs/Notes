Say you have this molecule (aniline)
![[Pasted image 20260525093535.png|439]]
It will have [[Mesomeric-effects (M-effects)]], meaning the shifts in ortho, meta, and para positions are different. This is dependent on if the NH2 group donates or withdraws electrons from the ring. NH2 donates lonepair, thereby you get:
![[Pasted image 20260525093827.png]]
This means electron density is higher at ortho and para positions. Thereby their shift will be lower than meta in this case.
$$\delta_{meta}>(\delta_{para}>\delta_{ortho})$$
e.g for a specific case, but para and ortho can vary:
$$7 > 6.6 > 6.5$$
M-effects are different for withdrawing: 
![[Pasted image 20260525094143.png]]
Where the shift change is
$$(\delta_{meta}<\delta_{para})<\delta_{ortho}$$
e.g for a specific case, but para and meta can vary:
$$7.5<7.6<8.1
