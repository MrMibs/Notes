The gyromagnetic ratio $\gamma$ is a constant specific to each nuclide (isotope). Also called proportionality factor. It tells us about energy difference between alpha and beta state.

It relates magnetic moment to angular momentum:

## Gyromagnetic ratio (γ)
$$
\mu = \gamma P
$$

where:
- $\mu$ = magnetic moment
- $P$ = spin angular momentum

##

Unit: $\frac{rad}{T\cdot S}$

Key idea:
- $\gamma$ is different for each nucleus
- It determines how strongly a nucleus interacts with a magnetic field / how sensitive it is to spin
- Converts spin to energy
