#nmrms
NOSEY shows cross peaks within $\leq 5Å$ internally and across atoms.

Same pulse sequence as [[COSY sequence]]. It works by 3 90 deg pulses then the FID
![[Pasted image 20260527184117.png]]

Really useful for specific applications such as stereochemistry, where the distances can between atoms can be determined:
![[Pasted image 20260527185843.png]]
In theory it is possible to do on entire proteins but it results in 1000s of distances which gives 3D structure with a lot of work. Another example is how things are placed inside cyclodextrines.
