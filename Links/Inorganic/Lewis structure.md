Rules:
1. Count the total number of valence electrons.
2. Identify the central atom: the least electronegative, except H
3. Place an electron pair between the central atom and each of the surrounding atoms and add lone pairs to the surrounding atoms.
4. If the octet rule is not respected and there are “leftover electrons”, add to them to the lone pairs to the central atom. If the octet rule is not respected and there NO MORE electrons are left, construct double and triple bonds using lone pairs from the surrounding atoms.
