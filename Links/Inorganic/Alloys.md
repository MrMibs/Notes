#inorganic 
There are two types of alloys:
Solid solutions : metals blend to form a homogeneous mixture. requirements: (a) metal atoms should have similar size, (b) metals must have the same structure.
![[Pasted image 20260610104722.png]]
Further, Intermetallic compounds: formation of precise stoichiometric phases. For example, copper and zinc form three “compounds”: CuZn, Cu5Zn8, and CuZn3

Magnetic properties depend on the atoms used:
![[Pasted image 20260610104803.png]]
Here we can see magnetic susceptibility depends on temperature.

Also magnetism exists as para or ferro. Para is where you can align spins with or against a magnetic field making them respond to magnets. Ferromagnetism is where spins are ordered in domains and you can permanently align the domains to a magnetic field.
