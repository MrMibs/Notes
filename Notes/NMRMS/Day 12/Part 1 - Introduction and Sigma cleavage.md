Useful for small volatile molecules e.g. exhaust pipe. Boiling point around 200-300 degrees. To do this we have the ![[MS at AAU#Agilent Intuvo GC-MS/MS Triple Quadrupole]]
GC/MS is mostly covered in this course. EI-MS is a sample bombarded by high energy electrons knocking off electrons from the sample and these (radical) ions are sent to mass analyzer (repetition). This high energy creates fragmentation.
