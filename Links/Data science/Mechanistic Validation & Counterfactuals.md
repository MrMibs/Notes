## Mechanistic Validation
## Meaning
Checking whether a model matches known real-world mechanisms.

## Key idea
Model should align with domain theory, not just fit data.

---

## Counterfactual Analysis
## Meaning
“What would have happened if X were different?”

## Example
- What if a patient had not taken a drug?

## Key idea
Simulates alternative realities to test causal claims.
