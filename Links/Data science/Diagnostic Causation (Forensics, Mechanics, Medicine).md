#datascience
## Definition
Diagnostic causation models infer the **most likely cause of observed symptoms or outcomes** using evidence and causal reasoning.

## Core idea
“What explanation best accounts for the observed data?”

## Process
1. Observe symptoms
2. Generate candidate causes
3. Evaluate consistency with evidence
4. Select most plausible cause

## Example domains
- Forensics: cause of death or fire
- Mechanics: engine failure diagnosis
- Medicine: disease diagnosis from symptoms

## Key intuition
This is **reverse causal reasoning**: from effects back to causes.

## Use cases
- Clinical diagnosis
- Fault detection
- Investigation systems
