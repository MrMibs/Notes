Application for EI-MS

Useful for small volatile molecules e.g. exhaust pipe. Boiling point around 200-300 degrees. To do this we have the ![[MS at AAU#Agilent Intuvo GC-MS/MS Triple Quadrupole]]
GC/MS is mostly covered in this course. EI-MS is a sample bombarded by high energy electrons knocking off electrons from the sample and these (radical) ions are sent to mass analyzer (repetition). This high energy creates fragmentation.
![[Pasted image 20260603122956.png]]
We create radicals, where they sit is predictable to some degree.
![[Pasted image 20260603123204.png]]
Resolution also falls to lower mass accuracies. This is sufficient for EI-MS. In basic basics a sigma bond is a bond between two adjacent atoms.
![[Pasted image 20260603123304.png]]
Say we had methane
![[Pasted image 20260603123330.png]]
Here we can see that EI induces sigma cleavage knocking off some H's. When deciding what we knock off we need to follow the **even-electron rule**, which says:
![[Pasted image 20260603123433.png]]
e.g. odd electron radicals can eliminate radicals or neutral species but even ions may only eliminate neutral species. We also cannot successively lose radicals. Applied to methane:
![[Pasted image 20260603123842.png]]

The fragmentation pattern seen is based on **ion stability**.
