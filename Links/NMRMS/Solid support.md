Solid support exists combined with ionization methods FAB, SIMS, and [[Matrix-Assisted Laser De-sorption or Ionization (MALDI)]], we focus mostly on MALDI. We basically have a sample deposited in a solid support. You then bombard the solid with a gas, generating ions. MALDI instead requires you to put a liquid sample inside a matrix, then let it dry and put the crystal inside the MS.
