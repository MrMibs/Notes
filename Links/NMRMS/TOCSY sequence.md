In TOCSY, the 2nd 90° pulse is replaced with a spin-lock pulse.2 The spin-lock is very interesting
(ya, ya, I know; maybe just to me): the spins are “fooled” into thinking that the large static
magnetic field has disappeared, and interactions – like coupling and chemical shifts – now depend
on the spin-lock strength, typically of about 12 kHz. That’s a lot smaller than 400 or 500 MHz.
J-couplings do not scale with the field strength, but chemical shifts do. All couplings during a spin-
lock are therefore strong. The strong coupling enables efficient mixing of all
protons in a coupled spin system.
