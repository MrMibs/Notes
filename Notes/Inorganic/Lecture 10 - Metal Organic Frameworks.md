#inorganic

Metal-Organic Frameworks (MOFs) are crystalline, periodic, highly porous (up to 94% empty space) frameworks

They are made using solvothermal, sonochemical, microwave-assisted or mechano-chemical methods, combining organic ligands and metal ions such as Co, Ni, Zr, Cr, Cu, Fe, Al, etc.

Framework self-assemble to form coordination bonds between transition metal cations and carboxylate anions
