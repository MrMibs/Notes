The Larmor frequency is the precession frequency of a magnetic moment in an external magnetic field without any interferance or anything, which otherwise would be added later.
## Larmor frequency equation
$$
\nu = \gamma B_0 \cdot \frac{1}{2\pi}
$$

where:
- $\nu$ = Larmor frequency \[1/s]
- $\gamma$ = [[Gyromagnetic ratio (γ)]]
- $B_0$ = [[Magnetic field strength (B)]]
## Angular Larmor frequency
$$\omega=2\pi\cdot\nu$$
where:
- $\nu$ = Larmor frequency
- $\omega$ is angular Larmor frequency \[rad/s]
##
In NMR, nuclei precess around the magnetic field at this frequency.

Derived like ($m_i$ is spin)
$$E=-\gamma \hbar m_I B_0$$
$$E=\pm \gamma\hbar \frac{1}{2} B_0$$
Thereby
$$E_\beta - E_\alpha = \gamma \hbar B_0$$
Since $\hbar = h/2\pi$
$$\nu = \frac{\Delta E}{h}=\frac{\gamma\hbar B_0}{h}=\gamma B_0 \cdot \frac{1}{2\pi}$$
We affect either effective magnetic field or energy difference to variations in frequency. Energy can equally be converted to frequency like:
$$\nu = \frac{\Delta E}{h}$$
