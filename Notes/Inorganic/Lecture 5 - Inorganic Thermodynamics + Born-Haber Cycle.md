To validate our model, the enthalpy of reaction 1 should be experimentally determined. However this is not possible because NaCl does not vaporize to give Na+ and Cl – ions! To circumvent this problem we will use the Born-Haber thermodynamic cycle.

$Na (s) + ½Cl_2(g) \rightarrow NaCl(s)$

Can be “broken down” into a series of steps. The sum of the enthalpies of these steps is equal to the enthalpy for the overall reaction. This is a consequence of the Law of Conservation
