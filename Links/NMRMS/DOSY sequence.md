#nmrms
Chemical shift vs diffusion coefficient.

DOSY tells you:

 **Molecular size (indirectly)**  
→ big molecules diffuse slowly (small D)  
→ small molecules diffuse fast (large D)
**Mixture separation without physically separating compounds**  
→ each compound often appears at a different diffusion “level”
**Aggregation / binding**  
→ if two species diffuse together, they likely interact

A DOSY spectrum tells you how different species in a mixture move in solution, i.e. their **diffusion coefficients**, which relate to **size and aggregation**
