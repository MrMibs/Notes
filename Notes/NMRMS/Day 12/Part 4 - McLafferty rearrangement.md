#nmrms 

General structure of this rearrangement:
![[Pasted image 20260603134653.png]]
It has requirements:
1. A, B, and D must be either carbon or heteroatoms (O, N, H)
2. A and B must me connected through a double bond
3. At least one $\gamma$-H is available (3 away from reference group)
4. B-H bond must be within 1.8Å and in the same plane
