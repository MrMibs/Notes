#nmrms
The Larmor frequency is the precession frequency of a magnetic moment in an external magnetic field.

![[Larmor frequency (ν)#Larmor frequency equation]]

This means the $\gamma$-value, which is constant for a given nuclei in certain conditions, will be the same, as we keep magnetic field strength constant. e.g.

15N is 61MHz
13C 150MHz
31P 243MHz
19F 565MHz
1H 600MHz

If you zoom in on this however, you see environment has an influence on the signal! These spectra have [[Chemical shift (PPM)]] on the x-axis and intensity on the y-axis. Information comes from
1. Exact [[Larmor frequency (ν)]]
2. [[Scalar-coupling]]
3. Area integral (proportional to atom amount)
