#nmrms 

Natural product chemistry requires an organism. We can study what they produce. 
1. You can then extract metabolites by using organic solvents or other methods for pulling out metabolites. 
2. We can then do LC-MS Analysis and Purification. 
3. We can dissolve the purified sample in a deuterated solvent so we can do 1- and 2 dimensional NMR. 
4. MS features also gives hints to what is going on through chemical formula, fragmentation patterns, neutral loss etc.
5. We can identify structures such as primary metabolites
