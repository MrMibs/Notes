#nmrms
DEPT is an acronym for "distortionless enhancement by polarization transfer"
![[Pasted image 20260525152436.png]]
Where $\theta$ gives different spectra:
$$\theta = 45\deg \quad \text{DEPT-45} \quad \text{Shows: }CH,CH_2,CH_3 \cancel C$$
$$\theta = 90\deg \quad \text{DEPT-90} \quad \text{Shows: }CH,\cancel {CH_2},\cancel {CH_3},\cancel{ C}$$
$$\theta = 135\deg \quad \text{DEPT-135} \quad \text{Shows positive: }CH_3, CH, \cancel C \quad \text{Shows negative: }CH2$$
