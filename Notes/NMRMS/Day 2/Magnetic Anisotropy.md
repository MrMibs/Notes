[[Magnetic Anisotropy]] occurs when electrons are not distributed spherically symmetrically (isotropically). This effect is greater with more electrons. Example:

From this, it is clear that CH2 is de-shielded and benzene further de-shielded. This is directional, as you move the electrons from one place to another, shielding a specific direction examples below:
![[Pasted image 20260525102440.png]]
Where + indicates more shielding (and thereby more electrons) and - indicates less shielding. Stronger for oxygen in the second example and flipped for triple bonds. Even more extreme for Aromatic rings where circulating π-electrons create a strong ring current. Protons outside the ring become deshielded.. Occurs even without pi-electrons!
