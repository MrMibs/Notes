#inorganic 
To predict bond geometry we need hybridization. We know:
- A covalent bond is formed by pairing the unpaired electrons in neighboring atoms
- The spins of the unpaired electrons must be antiparallel
![[Screenshot 2026-06-09 121018(1).png]]

To provide enough unpaired electrons in each atom for the maximum bond formation, electrons can be excited to fill empty orbitals during bond formation. The shape of the molecule results from the directions in which the orbitals of the central atom point. All the angles and so of hybridization are explained below:
