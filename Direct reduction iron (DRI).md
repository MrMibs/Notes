More CO2 friendly but requires high purity iron :(

An increasing proportion of iron is produced by direct reduction from the ore in the solid phase. high-purity iron ore must be used. Carbon monoxide and hydrogen, the reductants, are passed over heated iron ore.

Fe3O4(s) + CO(g) $\rightarrow$ 3 FeO(s) + CO2 (g)
Fe3O4(s) + H2 (g) $\rightarrow$ 3 FeO(s) + H2O (g)
FeO(s) + CO(g) $\rightarrow$ Fe(s) + CO2 (g)
FeO(s) + H2 (g) $\rightarrow$ Fe(s) + H2O (g)

The reducing gases are obtained by methane re-forming.
Thus, the economics of the process depend on the price of natural gas.

CH4(g) + H2O(g) $\rightarrow$ CO(g) + H2 (g)
CH4(g) + CO2(g) $\rightarrow$ 2 CO(g) + 2 H2 (g)

Advantage: economic, no further purification
Disadvantage: product contains most element impurities from the natural
ore.
