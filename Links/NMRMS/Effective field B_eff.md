The effective magnetic field $B_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is the net magnetic field experienced by a nucleus in a chemical environment.

It differs from the external field $B_0$ due to electronic shielding:
## Effective field B$_{eff}$
$$
B_{\mathrm{eff}} = B_0 (1 - \sigma)
$$

where:
- $B_0$ = external magnetic field
- $\sigma$ = shielding constant
##
Shielding reduces the local field felt by the nucleus, causing chemical shift in NMR. Shielding constants are on the scale of 1/million
