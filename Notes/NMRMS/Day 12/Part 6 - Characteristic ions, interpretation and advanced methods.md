Characteristic ions:
Alcohols have almost no molecular peak and easily fragments. Also a loss of water (as a neutral loss) can be observed which is confusing. Here [[Part 5 - Derivatization]] can be useful for alcohols.

Aliphatic alkanes show ion series with a difference 14. Molecular ion -> first peak has a $\Delta$ 15 as it is terminal.
![[Pasted image 20260603150708.png]]

Aromatic systems show high stability and strong molecular ion peaks.

**Step by step for unknown compounds**
- Check appendix 12 and 13A
- Label the spectrum
- Find molecular peak and base peak
- Consider [[Rings and double bonds equivalence]] and N-rule

Spectral libraries are typically used such as NEST MS. Understanding fragmentation patterns helps us interpret the libraries.
