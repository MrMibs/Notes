[[Spin quantum number (I)]] is represented by [[Spin angular momentum P]], however it is a fundamental property existing even without kinetic energy (0 K)

1896 Zeeman noticed atomic absorption lines (absorbance lines on visible spectrum) would split into 3 under magnetic field

Everything has spin! Nuclei (protons (charge 1, spin 1/2) and neutrons (charge 0, spin 1/2)) as well as electrons (spin 1/2)

Nucleus spin is sum of protons neutrons that it consists of (not entirely true) e.g. 
1H = 1 proton = spin 1/2 $\uparrow$
2H = 1 proton 1 neutron = spin 1 $\uparrow \uparrow$ (but could be 0 if they $\uparrow \downarrow$)

Bad rules for guessing nucleus spin (guess 0 or not 0) (check tables)
even mass = I=0,1,2,3... only when even charge I=0 (as neutrons and protons like to form opposite pairs)
odd mass = I=0.5,1.5,2.5

For $^{12}_{6}\mathrm{C}$, $^{16}_{8}\mathrm{C}$, $^{32}_{16}\mathrm{C}$ I=0 (stealthy, undetectable)

We like I = 1/2 e.g. $^{1}\mathrm{H}$ $^{13}\mathrm{C}$ $^{15}\mathrm{N}$ $^{19}\mathrm{F}$ $^{31}\mathrm{P}$
We can work with I $\geq$ 1 e.g. $^{14}_{7}\mathrm{N}$ $^{17}\mathrm{O}$ (5/2)! Quadropolar nucli
