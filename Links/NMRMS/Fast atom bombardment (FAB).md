We bombard a sample with a particle beam consisting of gas generating ions similarly to [[Matrix-Assisted Laser De-sorption or Ionization (MALDI)]]. The sample is dissolved in a liquid (instead of solid) matrix and throw energetic Ar or Xe atoms at it. This forms protonated molecular ions M+H, M+Na or M-H.

Matrix absorbs most of the energy so sample doesnt get blown up. Thereby **soft ionization**. Works well in 300-6000 Da.

![[Pasted image 20260528150019.png]]

It is a worse MALDI, it is still fast and good for compounds that break down when heated and most compounds in general, it is simple but has a lot of cons.
- Many unwanted signals from matrix
- Analyte must be soluble in matrix
- Creates high charge states
- Low sensitivity
- No fragment library
