#datascience
## Definition
Selecting only data that support a desired conclusion while ignoring contradictory evidence.

## Why it is a problem
It creates a biased and misleading picture of reality.

## Example
Reporting only successful drug trials while omitting unsuccessful ones.

## Warning signs
- Data exclusions are poorly justified.
- Only favorable examples are shown.
- Results change when omitted data are included.

## How to avoid it
- Use all relevant data.
- Predefine inclusion criteria.
- Report both supporting and conflicting evidence.

## Key idea
A conclusion should be based on all the evidence, not just the convenient evidence.
