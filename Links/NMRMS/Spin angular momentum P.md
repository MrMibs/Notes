Angular momentum is quantized and is a fundamental property of particles such as nuclei and electrons.
## Spin angular momentum P
For a nucleus with spin quantum number I:
$$
\vec{P} = \sqrt{I \cdot (I+1)}\ \cdot\hbar
$$
where:
- ($\vec{P}$) = spin angular momentum
- ($I$) = [[Spin quantum number (I)]]
- ($\hbar = \frac{h}{2\pi})$ is the reduced Planck constant

This angular momentum exists even in the absence of classical motion (i.e. not due to physical rotation). 
## Spin angular momentum P in Z-direction
In the Z direction this can be calculated as:
$$P_z = m \cdot \hbar$$
where:
- (m) is the [[Magnetic quantum number (m)]]
- ($\hbar = \frac{h}{2\pi})$ is the reduced Planck constant
