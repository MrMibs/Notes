Step 1. Insert sample and let heating do its time. 2 min for organic solvents, 5 min for water. Longer for demanding applications.

Step 2. Wobbling as explained in [[The Probe - the Antenna of an NMR Spectrometer]].

Step 3. Adjust field homogeneity / shimming from [[Parts of an NMR Spectrometer; Shim, Magnet, and Lock system]]

Step 4. Adjust experimental parameters (we have standards). We need to describe pulse, pulse sequences, strength, FID time, resolution and so on.

Step 5. Receiver gain adjustment. We amplify the signal in the antenna on the probe. This is limited, we need to input this.

Step 6. Measure

Step 2-6 is automated.

A few parameters you might want to change are:
- Temperature (TE) 278-323K, 5-50 deg.
- Number of scans (NS) amount of scans. 1H runs 8 if you have a decent concentration. 64 is enough and if it isn't get a better sample. 13C uses NS=1024->16384 (24h).
- Spectral width SW: width of measurement
- Offset frequency O1P: center of measurement
