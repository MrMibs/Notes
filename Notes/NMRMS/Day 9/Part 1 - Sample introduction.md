Analytes must be on gas phase when reaching the MS (ultra high vacuum) interior

Transition to gas phase may be optained:
- Prior to sample introduction (gasses)
- During sample introduction (volatiles)
- In the ion source (liquids and solids)

Often used with:
- [[Direct injection]] (infusion)
- [[Solid support]] (assisted solid $\rightarrow$ gas transition)
- [[Chromatographic interface]] (analyte separation)
